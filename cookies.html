Cookie Policy

How Lucent uses cookies and similar technologies — and the choices you control.

Version: 1.0 · Effective: 29 May 2026 · Last updated: 25 July 2026

Plain-language summary

Right now, our website at https://www.lucent-ai.app sets no cookies at all. No analytics, no advertising trackers, no session cookies — nothing is stored on your device when you browse this site. Joining the waitlist sends your name and email to our server (see the Privacy Policy), but that does not place a cookie either.

If we introduce cookies later — for example, sign-in or analytics — anything beyond the strictly necessary will only run if you say yes, and we will update this policy and show you a consent banner first.

1. What is a cookie?

A cookie is a small text file stored on your browser when you visit a website. Cookies let the site remember things about you — that you're logged in, your language preference, which features you've used. Some cookies expire when you close your browser (session cookies); others stay on your device for a set period (persistent cookies).

This policy also covers local storage, session storage, pixels, and (in mobile apps) SDK identifiers. We call all of these "cookies" in this policy for simplicity.

2. Cookies we use today

None. This website is a static informational site with a waitlist form. It does not set any cookies, does not use local or session storage, and does not load any analytics, heatmap, session-replay, or advertising scripts. There is no sign-in on the website today.

Submitting the waitlist form sends your name and email address to our server over an encrypted connection; how we handle that data is described in the Privacy Policy. No cookie is involved.

3. What will change before we introduce cookies

As the product grows, we expect to add features that need cookies — for example, signing in to a web account, remembering preferences, analytics to understand how the site is used, or measuring marketing campaigns. Before any non-essential cookie runs:

  • We will update this policy with a table of the specific cookies, their providers, purposes, and lifetimes.
  • We will show a consent banner where you can accept all, reject all, or pick categories individually — with "Reject all" as easy to find as "Accept all".
  • Strictly necessary cookies (such as keeping you signed in or remembering your consent choices) will be the only category that runs without consent.
  • Analytics and marketing cookies will be opt-in, consented separately.

4. Mobile apps — SDKs and identifiers

When our mobile apps launch, they will not use browser cookies, but they may use:

  • Strictly necessary SDK identifiers to keep you logged in and protect the app.
  • Analytics SDKs to understand how features are used. These will run only if you opt in.
  • Crash and diagnostic SDKs to fix bugs. These collect only device and crash data, not personal content.
  • Advertising identifiers (Apple's IDFA, Android's Advertising ID) — these will be off by default and only used if you opt in.

On iOS, we will follow Apple's App Tracking Transparency rules: any tracking that crosses to other companies' apps or sites only runs after you grant permission through the system prompt.

On Android, we will honour the Advertising ID opt-out and keep our Google Play Data Safety form in sync with this Cookie Policy and the Privacy Policy.

5. Managing cookies in your browser

Even though this site sets no cookies today, you can always manage cookies through your browser settings. The exact path depends on the browser — Chrome, Safari, Firefox, and Edge all expose cookie controls under Privacy or Settings.

6. Third parties

No third party sets cookies or receives data about your visit through this website today. If we introduce analytics or marketing providers later, they will be named in this policy — with their purposes, data, and retention — before their cookies run, and you will be asked for consent first.

We will not sell data collected by cookies, and we will not transfer it outside the UAE except under the safeguards described in the Privacy Policy §9.

7. Children

We will not knowingly use analytics or marketing cookies for visitors we know to be under 18. When accounts launch, minors' accounts will get stricter defaults, including disabled marketing identifiers.

8. Changes to this Cookie Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time — for example, when we add a third-party provider. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page shows the latest version; earlier versions are available on request from support@lucent-ai.app.

If a change introduces cookies that require your consent, we will ask for that consent before the new cookies run.

9. How to contact us

For any question about cookies:

  • Email: support@lucent-ai.app
  • Postal address: Dubai International Financial Centre (DIFC), Dubai, United Arab Emirates

You can also exercise the broader privacy rights described in our Privacy Policy at the same contact.
