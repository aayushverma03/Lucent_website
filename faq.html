FAQ

Questions, answered.

Everything you need to know about Lucent — recording drills, AI scoring, scouting, and signing.

What is Lucent?

Lucent is a football performance platform. You film drills from your phone, our AI scores your touch, speed, and control after every rep, and your stats build a verified record that scouts trust. When your numbers match a club's recruiting brief, they find you.

Do I apply to trials, or do clubs find me?

Clubs come to you. We show live recruiting briefs from clubs looking for your role, with a fit score so you know where you're a strong match. Upload the evidence a club asks for and, if your numbers fit, you appear on their shortlist.

I'm under 18 — can I sign up?

Yes, with a parent or guardian's consent. Guardians manage connections on the player's behalf, and we apply stricter privacy defaults for under-18 accounts. Coaches and scouts cannot contact a minor directly outside the guardian-mediated flow.

When does Lucent launch?

We're rolling out in waves. Join the waitlist and you'll be among the first to get access when we open up. Early members also get input into which features ship next.

Do I need a coach or special equipment?

No. A phone and a pitch are enough. Film any drill, any angle — our AI handles the analysis. You don't need a coach to use Lucent, but you can connect with one if you want a human in the loop.

Who can see my training videos and stats?

Your profile and data are private by default. Coaches and scouts only see your data after you accept a connection — and you can revoke it at any time. We never share your videos or health data with anyone outside the people you approve. Full details in our Privacy Policy.
